This isn't censorship. It's a dial.

Most blockers work like a wall: block the domain, hide the app, all-or-nothing. AuraShield works like a dial. You tell it what wears you down, and set how far to push it — from a soft peek-through blur up to fully hidden. Nothing is deleted, nothing is decided for you.

Gentle

A light blur that clears when you hover or focus it — a preview, not a wall.

Balanced

Full blur with a label and a "view anyway" button. One click reveals it.

Strict

Fully hidden — but it collapses into a slim, clickable strip. Nothing vanishes without a trace.

Everything runs on this device. Topic understanding uses Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano model, running locally. No page you read is ever sent anywhere — that's true whether the on-device model is on or off.
